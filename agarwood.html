The Nature & Value of Agarwood Trees

Agarwood, derived from the Aquilaria tree, is one of the most prized natural forest products in existence. Known as "Wood of the Gods," its rich aromatic resin yields rare Oud oil.

Our plantation uses environmentally friendly Thailand drip irrigation technology to ensure deep root nourishment, robust trunk growth, and high density resin yields naturally without synthetic damage.

  • High resin density heartwood development
  • 100% natural organic cultivation methods
  • Complete client tree allocation and certificates
