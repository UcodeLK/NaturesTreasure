Managed Plantation Service

How Our Managed Tree Program Works

A simple, transparent path to owning managed Agarwood trees and earning sustainable green returns.

01

Select & Allocate

Choose your desired quantity of Aquilaria (Agarwood) saplings. Each tree is geotagged and registered directly under your ownership certificate.

02

Thailand Eco-Nurturing

Our plantation experts utilize environmentally friendly Thailand drip irrigation to deliver nutrient-dense hydration directly to the root structure 24/7.

03

Natural Resin Induction

At maturity, we apply proprietary non-toxic organic eco-induction methods to stimulate natural dark resin formation without harming tree health.

04

Harvest & Distillation

We harvest the resinous heartwood and distill pure Oud oil in our artisanal copper stills. You receive your share of pure oil or financial yield in LKR.

Innovative Irrigation Tech

Specialized Thailand Environmentally Friendly Drip Irrigation

Traditional resin stimulation often damages trees with synthetic acids. Natures Treasure partners with leading agricultural engineers from Thailand to implement state-of-the-art eco-drip and automated misting systems.

Precision Root-Zone Hydration

Delivers micro-doses of organic bio-nutrients directly to the root base, accelerating trunk girth and healthy wood density.

Climate-Adaptive Misting
