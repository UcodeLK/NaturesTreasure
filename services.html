Managed Plantation Service

How Our Managed Tree Program Works

A simple, transparent path to owning managed Agarwood trees and earning sustainable green returns.

01

Select & Allocate

Choose your desired quantity of Aquilaria (Agarwood) saplings grown from high-quality first-generation Thailand seeds. Each tree is geotagged and registered directly under your ownership certificate.

02

Thailand Inoculation Tech

Our plantation experts utilize Thailand Inoculation Technology to induce high-grade resin formation cleanly and naturally.

03

Natural Resin Induction

At maturity, we apply proprietary non-toxic environmental eco-induction methods to stimulate natural dark resin formation without harming tree health.

04

Harvest & Distillation

We harvest the resinous heartwood and distill pure Oud oil in our artisanal copper stills. You receive your share of pure oil or financial yield in LKR.

Inoculation Technology

Thailand Agarwood Inoculation Technology

We use a specialized agarwood inducer developed and sourced from Thailand for controlled resin formation in agarwood trees. The technology is designed to stimulate the tree’s natural defence response, encouraging the development of resin-rich wood within targeted areas.

Combined with proper drilling, application, timing, and tree management, our inoculation approach is focused on achieving consistent resin development and high-quality agarwood production.
