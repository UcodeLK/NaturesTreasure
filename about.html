Founder’s Message

Mr. Ilham Nayeem

Founder & Managing Director, Natures Treasure

“Every tree we nurture is a promise to the future — a harmonious union of ethical forestry, scientific innovation, and generational wealth.”

I founded Natures Treasure with a deep reverence for the natural world and an unwavering conviction that rare luxury, scientific rigor, and environmental stewardship must walk hand in hand. What began as a personal passion for the sacred heritage of Agarwood has evolved into an enduring enterprise dedicated to re-imagining how pure Oud is cultivated, preserved, and cherished across the world.

Our vision extends far beyond traditional commerce. We have pioneered a transparent, technologically advanced agro-forestry model that bridges Thailand’s premier bio-inoculation science with Sri Lanka’s uniquely fertile terroir. By protecting each sapling and overseeing every stage from organic enrichment to artisanal distillation, we ensure that every tree yields its highest natural potential while enriching the land and local communities.

I have always believed that when we treat nature with patience, discipline, and integrity, it yields extraordinary value. Every plantation package we manage, every carefully nurtured tree, and every drop of pure Oud essence we produce stands as a testament to our transparent promise to our partners and clients.

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains absolute: to build a legacy of sustainable prosperity, environmental restoration, and artisanal perfection that will inspire generations to come.

Technical Partner’s Message

Mr. Witsawa Sripetkla

Agarwood Specialist & Technical Partner, Thailand

“Agarwood is more than a valuable natural resource. It is a connection between nature, knowledge, patience, and generations of experience.”

My journey with agarwood has been built through many years of learning, observing, working with trees, and understanding the importance of proper cultivation, inoculation, and sustainable harvesting. Through this experience, I have had the opportunity to work with growers and partners who share the same vision of developing agarwood in a responsible and professional way.

My partnership with Natures Treasure is built on this shared vision. We aim to bring practical agarwood knowledge and proven field experience from Thailand to Sri Lanka, while respecting the unique natural conditions and potential of Sri Lankan-grown agarwood.

Our goal is not simply to plant trees. We want to develop a complete and sustainable agarwood industry—from healthy planting material and proper tree management to inoculation, harvesting, processing, and the production of high-quality agarwood and oud oil.

I believe Sri Lanka has a great opportunity to become an important part of the international agarwood industry. With the right knowledge, technology, dedication, and respect for nature, we can create long-term value for farmers while protecting the future of this precious resource.

I am proud to work alongside Natures Treasure and to share my experience with Sri Lankan growers and the wider agarwood community.
